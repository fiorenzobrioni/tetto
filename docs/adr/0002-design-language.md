# ADR 0002: The family's design language, and Tetto's mark (Phase 0)

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-05

## Context

The owner asked for Tetto's typography, UI and UX to follow the sister apps', so the family is
recognisable at a glance. Tempo carries Chiaro's design language as code, through Passo (Tempo
ADR 0002): the generated dresses, the type, the shapes, the motion, the page gutter, the widget
colours, the accessibility checks. Copying that code is the shortest way to the same look, and
keeps one drawing of each thing in the family.

## Decisions

1. **The family's design system, copied** into `:core:designsystem` from Tempo
   (`tempo@6ce51eb`): `Scheme.kt` (Chiaro's two generated dresses, Paper and Vivid, never
   hand-edited: generated by Chiaro's `tools/gen_scheme.py` from its source colors, copied into
   Passo from `chiaro@309c0e3e`, into Tempo from Passo and into Tetto from Tempo unchanged; when
   Chiaro regenerates, they are copied again), `Type.kt` (Google Sans by default, Inter, the
   phone's sans; the same scale; the hero and the reading styles, tabular), `Shape.kt`,
   `Motion.kt` (springs, a 100 ms fade under reduced motion, read live), `PageWidth.kt` (the
   centred column on an open foldable; Tempo's two panes are not carried: Tetto has no page that
   needs them), `WidgetPalette.kt` (Chiaro's six card colours, hex for hex), `DenseTargets.kt`,
   the settings rows, `StatusCard`, `RevealItem`. Vivid is the default and dynamic colour is off
   by default, as in the family. Both fonts are the same files (OFL, credited in `licenses/`).
2. **Semantic colours only when a phase needs them.** Only `attention` comes over (the family's
   amber, 7:1 on either surface), for a cap spending ahead of its period's days. Over a cap is
   said in words, never by a colour alone; an ink for it, if Phase 2 needs one, comes with its
   contrast test.
3. **The hero is the amount.** `heroNumber` is Chiaro's temperature, Passo's step count, Tempo's
   clock; in Tetto it is the amount being typed on the numpad, tabular so the figures do not
   wobble as they change. Every amount is tabular (`tabular()`).
4. **Icons**: `TettoIcons`, the family's line icons (drawn in code at Material Symbols'
   outlined weight); the caps' curated icons join it in Phase 2, Material Symbols (Apache-2.0)
   drawn the same way and credited in `licenses/`.
5. **The launcher mark** (`tools/draw_launcher_icon.py`): the family's ring (radius 21, stroke
   10, on the warm white `#F7F4EE`), cut by an amber emblem as the sisters' are: Chiaro's sun at
   the upper right, Passo's shoe print at the upper left, Tempo's clock face at the lower left,
   Saldo's coins at the lower right; and **Tetto's roof at the lower right too**, where Saldo's
   coins stand (owner, 5 Nov 2026: the two money apps share the place, and a reader most often
   installs one of them). A roof at twelve o'clock over the ring would read as the generic
   "home" glyph of launchers and smart-home apps; as an emblem on the ring it reads as the app's
   mark. The roof stands upright: two slopes and a chimney, each a heavy stroke (6.2 units) with round ends (the
   family's emblems moved to round ends on 18 Oct 2026), cut out of the ring with the sisters'
   3-unit gap, inside the launcher's safe circle (its edge at 31.4 of 33). The ring shades
   clockwise from just past the roof, light to dark. **Its colours are the owner's**: they must
   stand clearly apart from Saldo's (sea green and soft brick, the same place on the ring) and
   from the other sisters' (Chiaro's sky to violet and indigo, Passo's greens, Tempo's azure to
   cobalt), under deuteranopia too. The free hues are few (blue-reds to purples, away from
   Saldo's orange-red brick and Chiaro's violet), and a peach, rose and berry ring was once
   turned down by the owner for Tempo. Two proposals were drawn side by side with the four
   sisters' marks, on light and dark grounds, under deuteranopia and in grey: raspberry to deep
   plum, and **fuchsia to wine** (`FF9AD5` to `E0218A` to `7A0B4E`), the owner's choice (5 Nov
   2026). The monochrome layer restates the same shapes for themed icons. The
   first run shows no icon (the family's onboarding frame).
6. **Tests carried over**: `TypographyTest` (every Material role follows the typeface setting),
   `ContrastTest` (every text ink at 4.5:1 on every ground, in both dresses and themes),
   `PageWidthTest` (the column's gutter), and the shared UI checks of `:core:testing`
   (`assertAccessible`, `walkPage`), which every screen's tests run at twice the text size and
   on an open foldable.

## Consequences

- A change of Chiaro's schemes or fonts reaches Tetto by hand, as it reaches the sisters. The
  code does not name the sister apps (the family's rule): the provenance lives in this record.
- Two fonts travel in the APK (about 1.2 MB), whatever the setting says.
- The widgets are drawn by the launcher in the system face whatever the setting says, as the
  family's are.
